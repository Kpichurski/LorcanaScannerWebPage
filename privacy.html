CardFrame Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information the CardFrame mobile app for iOS and Android collects, why we collect it, who processes it on our behalf, and the choices you have. CardFrame is a trading card scanner, price checker and collection manager. In this policy, "CardFrame", "we", "our" and "us" refer to the developer of the app, Happread, who is responsible for your data. You can contact us at admhappread@gmail.com.

Summary

  • You do not need an account. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Camera images stay on your device. To recognize a card, the app sends only numerical descriptors computed from the card image, never the image itself.
  • Your collections are stored on our servers, linked to a random identifier created by the app.
  • We collect app usage statistics, crash reports and diagnostics to keep the app working and to improve it.
  • Payments are handled by Apple or Google. Subscription status is managed by RevenueCat.
  • We do not sell your data, we do not show ads and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Information We Collect

App identifier and device verification

When you first use online features, the app creates a random identifier for your installation and a secret credential, and registers them with our servers. Registration is verified with Google Play Integrity (Android) or Apple App Attest (iOS), which confirm that requests come from a genuine copy of the app on a genuine device. The identifier links your collections to your installation and protects our service against abuse. A recovery secret is kept in your device's backup so that your collections can be restored after you reinstall the app.

Card scanning

The camera is used only while the scanner is open. Card detection and the image analysis run on your device. To identify a card, the app sends numerical descriptors computed from the card image to our servers, together with your app identifier. These descriptors cannot be turned back into the photo. They are used only to answer the recognition request and are not kept afterwards. Camera images are not uploaded or stored by us.

Your collections

Collections you create are stored on our servers so they are available in the app: collection names, the cards you add, their quantities and variants, condition or grade, and any purchase price or custom price you enter. We also save the daily total value of your collections to show value history charts. This data is linked to your app identifier, not to your name.

Feedback

If you send feedback from the app, we receive your message together with your app identifier, the app version and the platform.

Usage analytics and diagnostics

We use PostHog (EU cloud) to understand how the app is used and to find and fix problems. PostHog receives:

  • actions in the app, such as screens opened, scanner sessions and their results (for example the number of cards found), paywall views and purchase steps;
  • crash reports, error reports and performance diagnostics, such as model loading times;
  • device and app information: device manufacturer and model, operating system version, app version, language, time zone, screen size, network type and mobile carrier;
  • an approximate location (country and city) derived from your IP address;
  • a random analytics identifier and your app identifier.

On Android, the app also reads the Google Play install referrer, which tells us which store listing or campaign led to the installation. This information is not used to identify you personally.

Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive your payment card details. RevenueCat manages your subscription status and receives your purchase history for the app (products, prices, dates and renewal status) and an anonymous identifier. The app also records purchase steps in our analytics.

Information stored only on your device

Some information never leaves your device: your settings (currency, language and scanner preferences), the free scan counter, onboarding progress and which prices are hidden behind the free plan. Collection exports (CSV/JSON) are created on your device and shared only where you choose to send them.

How We Use Information

  • To provide the app: card recognition, prices, collections, value history and Premium features. Legal basis in the EEA and UK: performance of our contract with you.
  • To keep the service secure: device verification, preventing abuse and enforcing usage limits. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in protecting the service.
  • To understand usage and fix problems: analytics, crash reports and diagnostics. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in maintaining and improving the app.
  • To manage purchases: subscription status and restoring purchases. Legal basis: performance of our contract with you.
  • To answer your feedback.

Service Providers

We share data only with providers that process it on our behalf and under our instructions:

  • PostHog (EU cloud): usage analytics, crash and error reports;
  • RevenueCat: subscription management;
  • Google (Google Play billing, Play Integrity, Play install referrer) and Apple (App Store, App Attest): payments and device verification, under their own terms;
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers: our servers, database and the delivery of card images.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, property or safety of our users or of CardFrame.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell or rent your data.
  • We do not show advertising in the app.
  • We do not collect your device's advertising identifier and we do not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.
  • We do not upload or store your camera images.

Data Retention

Your collections and their value history are kept until you delete them in the app or ask us to delete them. Uninstalling the app does not delete data from our servers. Feedback messages are kept for as long as we need them to handle your feedback. Analytics events, crash reports and diagnostics are kept only as long as needed for the purposes above and are then deleted according to the retention settings of our analytics provider. Recognition requests are not kept after they are answered.

Data Security

All communication between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Access to your collections requires the credential registered by your app, and registration is verified by Google or Apple. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

International Transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the European Union and the United States. Where required, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your Rights and Choices

  • You can delete individual cards or whole collections at any time in the app. Deleted collections are removed from our servers.
  • You can export your collections from the app.
  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its processing, by emailing admhappread@gmail.com. Because the app has no accounts, we may ask you to send feedback from the app so we can find the data linked to your app identifier.
  • You can withdraw the camera permission in your device settings at any time; scanning then stops working.
  • If you are in the EEA or the UK, you can also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Children's Privacy

CardFrame is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has used the app and you want their data deleted, please contact us.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes. We will post the new version on this page and update the "Last updated" date above.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at admhappread@gmail.com.
