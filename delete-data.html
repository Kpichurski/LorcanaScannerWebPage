Delete Your CardFrame Data

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This page explains how to delete data from CardFrame: TCG Card Scanner for Android and iOS, published by PikFrame. CardFrame has no user accounts, so your data is linked to an identifier created by the app on your device.

Delete collections in the app

  1. Open CardFrame and go to the Collections tab.
  2. Open the menu of a collection and choose Delete.
  3. Confirm. The collection and all cards in it are removed from our servers right away.

To remove single cards, open the collection and delete them there.

Ask us to delete all your data

  1. Send an email to admhappread@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my CardFrame data".
  2. So we can find the data linked to your installation, include your phone model, the names of your collections (if you have any) and roughly when you installed the app.
  3. We confirm by email when the deletion is done, within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • your collections, the cards in them and any prices, condition or grade you entered;
  • the value history of your collections;
  • feedback you sent from the app;
  • your app registration (the app identifier and its credentials);
  • analytics events, crash reports and diagnostics we can link to your installation.

What is kept

  • Records of purchases and subscriptions kept by Apple, Google and our subscription provider RevenueCat, as required for billing, tax and fraud prevention. You can also contact Apple or Google about the data they hold.
  • Data that cannot be linked to you, such as aggregated statistics.

Uninstalling the app does not delete data from our servers. After deletion, using the app again creates a new, empty registration. More information is in our Privacy Policy.
